# Single-CPU elapsed-time calibration for the galectin affinity comparison

Freeze before collecting new times or new held-out executions. Use all Table 1 compounds 5–17 from the archived Gal-1/Gal-3 panel and both receptors per target (1GZW/4Q26, 1KJL/3T1L). Compounds 5–16 are the published substituted triazole subset; include the 17 reference ligand in the principal analysis. Source, prepared ligands, fixed microstates, receptors and boxes are unchanged. One trajectory, one CPU, at most nine modes, energy range 100. Pin the complete serial run to CPU 7. No other runs of this experiment may overlap. Record complete CLI subprocess elapsed time, including process creation, map construction, calculation and output, and actual hunt/second/explicit joint energy-gradient evaluations.

Calibration uses only observed elapsed times, with seed 75467, in four randomized blocks per ligand/receiver containing parent and exact-linear engines at 3000 and 12000 nominal evaluations. Each combination is timed once; rotate the arm/cap order across shuffled ligand/receiver cells. For each cell, target the measured time for parent at 12000. If linear at 12000 already fits that time, select 12000. If its low-cap time already exceeds the target or its two calibrations are nonmonotonic and the high-cap time exceeds target, select 3000. Otherwise linearly interpolate between the linear measurements, round down, and clip to [3000,12000]. No measured Kd, pose or score is used in calibration.

Freeze all 52 caps before any validation run. On seed 98251 run parent at 12000 and linear once at its corresponding per-cell cap. Preserve timings, scores and raw poses from both phases. Also report the existing fixed-cap seed-98251 results separately for context: this seed has historically been evaluated and is held out only from timing calibration. No new chemical-series or statistically independent generalization claim follows.

Rank the original native first pose only. Lower predicted affinity score and lower Kd give concordance. Per receiver and equal-weight two-receiver consensus, compute Spearman, Kendall tau-b and exact ≥3-fold pair concordance with half credit for ties, also for the triazole and equal-heavy-atom subsets. No Poisson/binomial inference on pair counts. Report total and per-target time/evaluation ratios, distribution of cell ratios and fraction whose linear time is within ±5% of parent; any budget mismatch is material to interpretation. Call results approximately time-matched only when the target-level paired elapsed ratio is within 5%, with the true individual mismatch stated. A materially cheaper arm may be described as such without assigning full elapsed equality. The one-pair-per-cell validation measures noise as well as the calibration quality. Do not recalibrate on validation scores or timings.
