# Pair-derivative docking protocol

The primary panel was fixed before results were inspected. For each of ADORA2A (3EML/ZMA and 3UZA/T4G) and REN (1RNE/C60 and 2V0Z/C41), dock each graph-derived ligand into its own and the other ligand's receptor. Use each receiver's experimental ligand centroid only to define the pocket, including for cross-docking. No crystallographic ligand conformer is used as a docking start. Validate the complete stereochemical ligand graph and accept only receivers with successful Meeko typing, no omitted nonwater asymmetric-unit atom within 8 Å and no rebuilt heavy atom within 8 Å. Do not tune model parameters on any structure. Additional ligands or receptors are not used as templates.

Both primary REN receiver preparations failed the valence check on reconstructed glutamate residues before running REN docking. A protocol amendment admitted the first deposited chains with one suitable ligand in 3G6Z/A7T and 3SFC/S53 as REN receivers and cognate controls under the same preparation gates. Keep the original molecules as cross-docking queries, test the fallback queries reciprocally where eligible and record all exclusions, including failed primary receptors. This amendment follows structural preparation, independent of any score, pose or arm-specific observation.

Primary four-way comparison uses source-built upstream Vina (fork base), unchanged production Pro, the piecewise-linear exact derivative, and the monotone cubic derivative. Use seeds 75467 and 98251, caps 3000 and 12000, one trajectory, one pinned logical CPU, a 24 Å cubic box, score window 100 kcal/mol, and at most nine returned modes. For Pro use native merge, native local optimizer and native explicit refinement defaults. In each cell all arms are sequential; cycle order by cell. Include process startup, input reading, precalculation, map construction and output in elapsed time. All arms reuse the identical upstream-independent input preparation. The original Vina search cap can be exceeded before the next trajectory step checks it. Count measured gradient/energy calls through search and explicit refinement, including observed overruns. A separate instrumentation-only upstream replay must serialize exactly the same output before accepting its counts.

Use symmetric heavy-atom receptor-frame RMSD at 2 Å for top-1, top-5, any returned pose, and top-1 conditional on a hit in the returned pool. For cross-docking, compare to the original pose transferred by rigid pocket-Cα alignment, using same-residue correspondences within 14 Å of both pocket centers; discard alignment RMSD over 2 Å or fewer than 12 atoms. Count ligand–protein heavy-atom contacts below 0.75 times the sum of element van der Waals radii. Seeds, receptors, ligands and caps repeat; do not calculate independent-binomial significance from these cells.

Timing control, frozen before inspecting RMSDs: for each candidate and query/receiver, compare its two timings on seed 75467 to the parent time at cap 12000. Interpolate the cap only downward to target that time, clipping to [3000,12000] and rounding down. If the endpoints are nonmonotone and the high-cap arm is slower than parent, use the low cap. Apply the chosen cap once on seed 98251. If cap 12000 or 3000 is selected, reuse its already measured result. Report achieved time errors, including misses, and the subset where candidate time and measured joint-evaluation count both do not exceed parent. A ≤5% time surplus is a secondary descriptive tolerance.
