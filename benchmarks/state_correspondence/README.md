# Fixed ammonium geometry and scaffold transfer in human CA II

An input PDBQT records an unspecified tertiary ammonium center in SMILES while a particular starting conformer realizes one geometric orientation. Reflecting this ligand gives the other orientation because the five inputs have no other stereoelement. The test asks whether filtering a scaffold transfer against that **realized** state improves selection of both the observed binding pose and the deposited orientation of the three heavy neighbors. The poses are two fixed alternatives for the same declared chemistry. The model assigns no solution population or proton-exchange rate to them.

The five crystal ligands, zinc-containing receptors 6WQ4/6WQ8, seeds 314159/271828, rotatable bonds, charges, native score and complete-case affinity filter are shared with the [hCA II experiment](../cutoff_switch/hcaii/README.md). Bonardi et al. measured the series by stopped-flow inhibition of CO₂ hydration at pH 7.5. The seven unique ≥3-fold Ki pairs occur in each of four receptor/seed combinations. [Structures and inhibition assays](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jmedchem.0c00733)

## Three paired searches

1. **Declared-graph control.** Preserve each historical independent donor. Enumerate maximum common substructures after reading the unspecified nitrogen declaration, preserving SDF atom order for coordinate transfer. Both geometric input states use this same complete set. This control uses 29, 34, 35, 34 and 35 common atoms, and 8, 8, 16, 8 and 16 assignments for 6WQ4, 6WQ5, 6WQ7, 6WQ8 and 6WQ9 respectively.
2. **Compatibility with full fallback.** For each query input state and complete assignment, use the N and its three heavy neighbors in PDBQT serial order. Compute the signs of the corresponding scalar triple products in the input and independent donor coordinates. Preserve complete mappings with equal signs and mappings with an incomplete neighborhood. Predefine the fallback to the full declared set when every mapping is incompatible. Generation reads neither query crystal geometry nor desired bound-state sign.
3. **Release the conflicting neighborhood.** After assignment inspection and before evaluating this arm, freeze a separate test for the all-incompatible case. For that state alone discard the N and its three directly bonded heavy-atom anchor points from every assignment, preserving all distal points and removing duplicate alternatives. This tests a local relaxation while the larger scaffold can still convey orientation information.

For all five ligands, the permutations differ away from the ammonium neighborhood. In each receptor the full set is compatible with exactly one state. That state is original for 6WQ4 and 6WQ9 and reflected for 6WQ5, 6WQ7 and 6WQ8. Thus ten receptor/ligand/state combinations contain only compatible mappings and ten invoke the all-incompatible fallback. These results concern the geometry transferred from each **other** compound, which can differ from the query's observed bound configuration.

Every cell combines two slots of two trajectories each, with a nominal 50,000 evaluations per trajectory. In the both-state comparison, the common slot searches original input and the second searches reflected input at seed+1. A second-original slot supplies the equal-budget original-only control. All 60 search slots in each new arm were actually run. The full-fallback arm generated byte-identical raw pose files in 60/60 slots compared with the declared control. The local-release arm matched in 28/60 slots. The two-state outcome for each arm is drawn from 20 receptor/ligand/seed cells.

## Results with both input states

A joint hit requires ligand RMSD ≤2 Å in the receptor frame **and** matching the nitrogen neighbor orientation of the deposited query. The pool column gives the number of cells for which at least one returned pose meets both requirements.

| Search | Final selection | RMSD hits / 20 | Joint first / 20 | Joint pool / 20 | Correct Ki / 28 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Declared or full fallback | Native energy | 5 | 0 | 10 | 13 |
| Declared or full fallback | Native plus scaffold prior | 9 | 6 | 10 | 12 |
| Declared or full fallback | Scaffold geometry alone | 12 | 9 | 10 | 11 |
| Release conflicting neighborhood | Native energy | 6 | 1 | 10 | 14 |
| Release conflicting neighborhood | Native plus scaffold prior | 7 | 2 | 10 | 14 |
| Release conflicting neighborhood | Scaffold geometry alone | 9 | 8 | 10 | 8 |

Retained-prior and geometry-only methods change which pose is read out. Their affinity reported here always uses that pose's native energy and a shared within-ligand intramolecular reference. The search prior is excluded from the affinity score.

The control's RMSD-only pool covered 12/20 cells and the local-release variant covered 10/20, while joint pool coverage was 10/20 in both. Compatible filtering alone provided no selection among the mappings offered by these particular donors. Removing the conflicting local geometry gave one additional native-selected joint hit alongside four fewer hits when selecting with the retained prior. The results provide no consistent improvement across these selection mechanisms. Ligand-to-ligand transferability of the bound nitrogen orientation and native final pose selection remain distinct limitations. All repeated conditions share the same five compounds.

## Reproduction and independent checks

Prepare the hCA II panel and bonds with the linked primary benchmark. Use the declared-graph mapping preparation and ensure the original unfiltered two-state control was generated from those exact files. In the commands below the three final directory names refer to newly generated full-fallback, released-neighborhood and archived results respectively.

~~~bash
python benchmarks/mapping_ensemble/prepare.py hcaii /tmp/fdv-second/prepared /tmp/fdv-declared-hcaii --declared-nitrogen
python benchmarks/cutoff_switch/hcaii/ammonium_states.py --work /tmp/fdv-second --output /tmp/fdv-map-states-declared --binary build/ensemble/vina --ensemble-anchors /tmp/fdv-declared-hcaii
python benchmarks/cutoff_switch/hcaii/rank_state_pools.py --runs /tmp/fdv-map-states-declared --prepared /tmp/fdv-second/prepared
python benchmarks/state_correspondence/experiment.py /tmp/fdv-second /tmp/fdv-declared-hcaii /tmp/fdv-declared-state-signed build/ensemble/vina
python benchmarks/state_correspondence/experiment.py /tmp/fdv-second /tmp/fdv-declared-hcaii /tmp/fdv-declared-state-relax build/ensemble/vina --release-conflicting-neighborhood
python benchmarks/state_correspondence/verify.py /tmp/fdv-second /tmp/fdv-declared-hcaii /tmp/fdv-declared-state-signed
python benchmarks/state_correspondence/verify.py /tmp/fdv-second /tmp/fdv-declared-hcaii /tmp/fdv-declared-state-relax --release-conflicting-neighborhood
python benchmarks/state_correspondence/compare.py /tmp/fdv-map-states-declared /tmp/fdv-declared-state-signed /tmp/fdv-declared-state-relax /tmp/fdv-state-comparison
~~~

The independent audit identified the center and neighbors using CCD bonds and atom names, recovered the **donor** sign in raw PDB chain A residue 303, and checked every retained point against the exact assignment. It recomputed all 538/539 candidate RMSDs and query bound signs with a separate RDKit/CCD path (maximum RMSD discrepancy <5.0 × 10⁻⁹ Å), independently evaluated every prior, and reconstructed 120 pose selections and 168 Ki credits per run set. A third comparison checks the complete raw output for bitwise identity against the declared-graph control.
