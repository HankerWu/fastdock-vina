# Predeclared Gal3 cost transfer on a fresh seed

Use all 47 molecules from Sörme et al. in both frozen 1KJL and 3T1L inputs. Use one unused Gal3 seed, 411119, for every production arm and cell. Fit no chemical outcome. Use no validation timings, poses or labels to tune caps. Train on measured parent and gated CLI wall times in the preceding affinity/cost.csv at seed 614039. Compute each candidate cap by flooring min(12000,12000*parent_seconds/gated_seconds) to a multiple of 500 evaluations. The 500-unit grid gives a conservative margin for timing noise and non-scaling map initialization. One cap applies to every ligand on a given receiver. For 1KJL the times 7.60555893 / 8.02521658 give cap 11000. For 3T1L the times 8.31327777 / 8.52571728 give cap 11500. Take no further calibration action even if the cap-to-time approximation fails on the new seed.

Use native parent at 12000 as primary reference. Run gated replacement and recurrence-gated replacement at those same two candidate caps. Include native parent at the two lower caps as an ablation separating shortening the trajectory from changing the pool policy. No arm receives additional predictive seeds, search trajectories, refinement or poses. Run all four arms in rotating order within the same randomized cells on the same pinned CPU, exhaustiveness 1, modes 9, energy range 100. Record actual full CLI time, joint hunt/second/explicit calls, actual explicit input slots, gained/lost outputs and saturation of each measured budget. Report the controller computation, shared preparation, older timing calibration and offline analysis separately.

Primary assessment is mean first native scores over the two receptor runs and ≥3-fold experimental Kd separation (403 unique pairs), with 0.5 ties. Verify against raw output, both single-receptor series, 35 matched size+charge pairs, ≥10-fold differences, pKd versus negative-score Spearman and Kendall tau-b, per-molecule leave-one-out sensitivity and complete pair flips. The one fresh seed is a test of numerical replication and cost transfer on an existing assay. It supplies neither independent chemotypes nor a new experimental series. Report any new-seed excess in time or joint calls; defer robust equal-cost or mechanistic claims if the evidence does not support them.
