# Post hoc, crystal-informed slot substitution

The primary RBP4 1RBP→3FMZ seed 85237 old candidate has identified a rejected close pose, ID20, and its original better-scoring 1.892166 Å neighbour ID18. This experiment is registered after their selection. Rerun the same candidate, CLI, inputs, proposal stream and CPU/12k limits, in two modes: untouched nine inputs as the control, or exactly one substitution, full ID20 search-state copy in the original slot of ID18 after the global merge sort. Other eight inputs, native refinement, common intramolecular reference, final scoring, sorting, filtering and PDBQT writer stay on the actual source call path. Check all 27 merge input states and search counters against the original, and check eight unchanged input states and emitted control file hash. Predict ID20 remains within 2 Å of the transferred reference after refinement; separately test whether it becomes rank 1 and whether its comparable apparent CLI score is lower than original ID18. This is an explanatory forced admission intervention. Neither choice nor result is blind evidence of an improved selection rule.

Two separate processes for the control and the substitution, repeated once each with a fresh working directory in reverse order. One CPU per execution, same caps as original. Save full refined factors, final ranks/coordinates, stage evaluation counts, logs, exact input and binary hashes, CPU/elapsed/RSS and commands. All four outcomes retained, including failures. Stop on upstream mismatch, invalid state identity, altered control pool, missing energy components, or inconsistent repeated released pools. No additional substitutions, radius sweeps, or promotion.
