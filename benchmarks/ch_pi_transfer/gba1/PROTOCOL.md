## Human lysosomal acid β-glucosidase transfer

Frozen before running search or viewing pose or energy results. This panel tests whether the published methane–benzene CH–π correction can identify better **whole-ligand** poses among unchanged FastDock Pro candidates for iminosugars. It is a new protein family for this branch experiment. Prior use of the structures to develop other methods is possible.

Use one occupied chain per deposition, the first alphabetically: 2NSX B / IFM (isofagomine), 2V3D A / NBV (N-butyl-DNJ), 2V3E A / NND (N-nonyl-DNJ). These three reversible inhibitors span a compact ring and two progressively longer tails. Run all three in each receptor, using the known binding site. Generate query starts from the CCD graph by ETKDG/MMFF. Primary microstate: protonate the piperidine nitrogen; sensitivity: the neutral free base on the same graph. These are conditional single-state experiments. Keep the same conventional Meeko receptor templates in both arms. Do not fit pKa, weights or binding free energies.

Use a 24 Å cube centered on the receptor's deposited ligand, the existing Late search with 4 trajectories, max_evals 12000 per trajectory, 40 refinement candidates and 9 output modes. Use seeds 43103, 43117 and 43133 from the repository cross-docking panel. Compare unmodified Pro and the fixed 0.3-weight literature CH–π reranker on exactly the same outputs; equal energies retain original order. Report missing predictions and *all* poses. No experimental coordinates enter selection. For comparisons between crystal frames, fit corresponding receptor Cα within 14 Å of their respective pocket centers (sequence residue number and residue identity); score against all complete chirality-compatible CCD mappings and deposited alternate positions without ligand fitting. Record the receptor fit residual and aligned Cα count.

Primary contrast is paired full-heavy-atom Top-1 RMSD and its mean and median for protonated ligands, with cognate and cross-docking shown separately. Also report quartiles, P90, P95, best@5, minimum released RMSD, regret, and changed winners. The returned-pool minimum is a lower bound on the geometric quality obtained after filtering, not the oracle over every search evaluation. Tabulate every condition and examine changed winners including decoys. Treat seeds and the three receptors as repeated observations from one protein. Report seed-block uncertainty conditional on this panel if estimable; no target-level generalization interval. Compare an official Vina 1.2.7 search on the protonated panel as a separate reference. Kd/Ki or free energy accuracy cannot be inferred from this structural panel.

Sources: [deposition 2NSX](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2NSX), [depositions 2V3D/2V3E and original co-crystal study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17666401/), [bound iminosugar protonation experiments](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32191378/), [CH–π model](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.3c00300).
